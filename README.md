The field changes every day. Keeping up means checking a dozen sites, and you still find out late.
**radar does the checking.** Every ten minutes it reads the labs, the papers, the repos and the crowd at once,
and opens with a short list: what to **try**, **read** and **watch** today, each with the first command ready to copy.

## What makes it different

- **Firsts, not hype.** radar finds the sentence where a release says what it made possible, and labels it honestly: **First**, **Now possible**, or just **Claims**.
- **One story, not ten tabs.** The same release on a lab blog, Hugging Face, Hacker News and Reddit becomes one card. Showing up in several places counts as evidence.
- **Traction you can trust.** Stars and likes per day on the actual repo or model, not the volume of the hype.
- **The unexpected lane.** Brand-new names, off-topic spikes, and strong papers nobody has upvoted yet. The thing least expected is often the one that matters.
- **See it, don't read it.** Figures, demo clips and previews first. One line of text per card.
